# Google Play listing: draft 1

Character limits per Play Console (**verify** before submission).

**App name (30):** `Habitrak: My Day Tracker` (24)

**Short description (80):** `Tap what you do, see your day. A one-tap day tracker for your home screen.` (74)

**Full description:**

> Habitrak shows you where your day actually goes.
>
> Put the My Day widget on your home screen. Tap Coffee when you have one. Tap Work when you start, tap again when you stop. That's it. No forms, no streaks, no judgment.
>
> **See your day as a picture.** Every tap lands on a 24-hour ribbon, so you can see the shape of your day at a glance.
>
> **Track what matters to you.** Coffee, water, walks, meals, work, screen time, sleep. Add your own. If you're cutting back on something, like smoking or drinking, track that too and set a ceiling that fits you.
>
> **See how things connect.** Your week view puts everything side by side: the coffee after a short night, the break you skip on busy days.
>
> **Private by design.** Your log stays on your phone unless you turn on backup. We never sell your data or use it for ads.
>
> Habitrak is for awareness and personal tracking. It is not medical advice or treatment.
